Goal

The goal of this seminar was to study recent work of Landesman-Litt on local systems of geometric origin over general curves. The main results are contained in LL22b and LL22c. We focused on the case of unipotent monodromy at the cusps to avoid the machinery of parabolic and coparabolic structures; a simplified version of the first paper is available as LL22a. The program is available as a PDF.
